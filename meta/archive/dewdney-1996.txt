Dewdney, Patricia; Michell, Gillian RQ , v35 n4 p520-23,526-36 Sum 1996 Librarians often have communication &quot;accidents&quot; with reference questions as initially presented. This article presents linguistic analysis of query categories, including: simple failures of hearing, accidents involving pronunciation or homophones, accidents where users repeat earlier misinterpretations to librarians, and accidents where users &quot;creatively reconstruct&quot; the terminology of their need. Restatement techniques and neutral and follow-up questioning are discussed.
