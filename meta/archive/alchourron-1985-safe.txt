151 Citations Explore all metrics Abstract This paper is concerned with formal aspects of the logic
of theory change, and in particular with the process of shrinking or contracting a theory to
eliminate a proposition. It continues work in the area by the authors and Peter Gärdenfors. The
paper defines a notion of “safe contraction” of a set of propositions, shows that it satisfies the
Gärdenfors postulates for contraction and thus can be represented as a partial meet contraction, and
studies its properties both in general and under various natural constraints. This is a preview of
