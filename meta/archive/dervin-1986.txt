Dervin, Brenda; Dewdney, Patricia RQ , v25 n4 p506-13 Sum 1986 Describes a strategy for query negotiation, based on a sense-making approach, which enables librarians to understand queries from users' viewpoint. The results of tests of the neutral questioning approach in workshops for librarians are discussed along with participating librarians' responses to this technique.
