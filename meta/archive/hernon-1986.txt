Hernon, Peter; McClure, Charles R. Library Journal , v111 n7 p37-41 Apr 15 1986 Reports results of unobtrusive testing of reference service (asking reference questions of library staff who are unaware of being evaluated) in government documents and central reference areas of 26 U.S. libraries. Accuracy of answers, interview and search process duration, referral, and use of catalog and technology are highlighted.
