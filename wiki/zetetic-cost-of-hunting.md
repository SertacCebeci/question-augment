# The zetetic cost of hunting

> **This wiki's inference, not a published result.** The property that lets an
> axis-hunting generator reach a dimension outside the model — that it does not
> serve the question currently being pursued — is exactly the property that
> makes it a distraction under ZIP. Hunting is therefore charged to the
> inquiry that owns the budget, and has to be opened as its own inquiry rather
> than interleaved with one.

## The inference

**Premise 1** ([[zetetic-instrumental-principle]], friedman-2020). An agent[^s1][^s2]
that has taken up a question under a deadline owes it the necessary means, and
those means include declining information that does not serve it — including
information that is free.

**Premise 2** ([[unknown-unknowns]], and the generator rows of Axes).
The stall-1 generators — [[grand-tour-question]], [[premortem]],
[[metamorphic-probes]], the metaquestion of [[context-free-questions]] — are
defined by *not* scoring candidates against the current model. They import
material the current question does not need; that is how they reach axes the
parameter space does not contain.

**Therefore.** Relative to the question currently adopted, every one of these
generators is the kind of act ZIP forbids. The two properties are the same
property, seen from the two sides.

Three consequences, which are this note's and neither Friedman's nor Falbo's:

- **Hunting is not a free background process.** Its price is not the token
  cost of one more question but the delay it imposes on the question that owns
  the means.
- **A hunt has to be its own inquiry** — its own question ("what is missing
  from my picture of $X$?"), its own means, its own stopping point. Folding it
  into the same selection loop asks one loop to serve two ends between which
  it has no exchange rate; ZIP supplies none, because Friedman sets competing
  zetetic ends aside (but see Limits). This is not
  [[divergent-convergent]] restated: that split separates producing from
  judging *inside* one adopted question, as a craft rule about when judgement
  degrades production, whereas the separation here is between two inquiries
  and is derived from who owes the means. It inherits the same open seam —
  neither framework says when to suspend — which the last Limits bullet
  concedes.
- **Falsifiable form.** An agent that interleaves hunting moves into a
  deadline-bound inquiry should resolve the original question later, and at a
  lower rate within the budget, than one that suspends the inquiry to hunt and
  then resumes. That is measurable on any benchmark with a fixed [[question-budgets|question budget]]; it has not been measured here.

Note that the conflict is *normative*, not merely budgetary: under
`zetetic-epistemic-separation` (draft dropped) the reason to hunt is a practical one about
what the inquiry is for, so it cannot be settled by any score computed inside
the current model.

## Transfer

| | |
|---|---|
| **In its home field** | ZIP governs a human inquirer under a deadline; the means are attention and time, and "distraction" is assessed intuitively against cases. |
| **Here** | The means is a question budget or a step budget; "distraction" has a sharp reading — a question whose answer does not fall inside the partition the current question opened ([[question-as-partition]]). |
| **What survives** | The structure: an adopted end plus a bounded means implies that acts not serving the end are charged to it. This needs no intuition, only that the budget is shared between the hunt and the inquiry. |
| **What does not** | ZIP's silence about competing ends transfers as a gap, not as a licence (the silence itself is the unconfirmed element — see Limits). The framework cannot rank hunting against answering, and this note does not supply the ranking either — it claims only that the two cannot be one loop. |

## Limits

- **One load-bearing element is unconfirmed against the primary text.**
  `friedman-2020` was read from the author's preprint, and the element this
  note leans on twice — that Friedman sets competing zetetic ends aside, so
  ZIP issues no ranking between them (the second consequence, and the
  Transfer's "what does not" row) — could not be confirmed against that text
  on 2026-09-11, because the PDF was unreadable by the fetch tool; it is
  corroborated only through secondary discussion. Every other element of
  `friedman-2020` used here was corroborated. If Friedman does adjudicate
  competing zetetic ends, the "no exchange rate" step weakens, and the
  consequence reduces to a budget claim.
- **The normative framing rests on a source this wiki has not read whole.**
  `falbo-2023` enters here only at second hand through
  `zetetic-epistemic-separation` (draft dropped); its full text could not be retrieved on
  2026-09-11 and that note's reading is from the published abstract (plus an
  unkeyed author interview). The closing "normative, not merely budgetary"
  sentence is therefore as thin as that dependency.
- The inference is only as strong as premise 1's deadline. With no time bound
  and no shared budget, free information is free and the conflict disappears;
  an architecture whose hunting runs in a *separate* process (a second agent,
  an offline pass) is outside the argument entirely — and is, in fact, what
  the argument recommends.
- Premise 2 rests on [[unknown-unknowns]]' generation/selection distinction,
  which that note names as an organising distinction and not a measured one.
  If a generator could be shown to serve the current question it would not be
  forbidden — and [[problem-solution-coevolution]] reports exactly that in
  design protocols, where a candidate answer produces requirements for the
  problem being worked on. The conjecture's scope is the case where the
  question is fixed and urgent.
- No source states this. It composes `friedman-2020` with rows of
  Axes that come from other fields, and those fields' authors have not
  been asked whether their generators are distractions.
- It says nothing about **how much** hunting to buy, nor when to suspend; that
  remains the open stall-1 problem of when the axis set is complete, and it is
  the same seam [[divergent-convergent]] leaves open.
