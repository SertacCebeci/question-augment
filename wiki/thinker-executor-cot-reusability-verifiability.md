# Thinker-Executor CoT reusability and verifiability

Aggarwal et al. ask how to evaluate chain-of-thought independent of accuracy on the target task.[^c1] They separate generation from execution: a Thinker produces the CoT and an Executor follows it to produce a solution.[^c2] Two scores come out, each averaged over a committee of Executors to reduce the bias of any single one.[^c3]

## Reusability

Only questions the Thinker answers correctly are kept. If the Executor is wrong alone, it is given the question plus the Thinker's CoT; becoming correct puts the question in a "helped" set. If the Executor is right alone, it is given a corrupted CoT (a plausible trace with a few steps altered to change the outcome); becoming wrong puts it in a "harmed" set.[^c4] The score is helped plus harmed over the Thinker-correct questions, times 100. A high score means the Executor ignores its own thinking and follows the supplied CoT. The authors therefore read reusability as persuasiveness: useful for teaching other agents, equally usable to lead an agent to a wrong answer.[^c5]

## Verifiability

Verifiability is the percentage of questions on which the Executor, given the Thinker's CoT, ends at the same final answer as the Thinker. A high score means the CoT is unambiguous and leads different interpreters to one conclusion.[^c6] The authors' analogy: reusability is the orator who persuades, verifiability is the legal document with little room for multiple readings.[^c7]

## What was measured

- Four Thinkers (Gemma3-27B, Llama3.1-8B, DeepSeek-R1-14B, Phi4-reasoning-14B), ten Executors of 360M to 3B parameters, five benchmarks (GSM8K, SVAMP, StrategyQA, ARC-Challenge, CommonsenseQA).[^c8]
- No Thinker is best throughout: with the full committee Phi leads reusability on GSM8K, Llama on SVAMP.[^c9]
- Scores rise with committee strength (Strong > Full > Weak), but Thinker rankings are stable across committees with a few strong Executors: Kendall's tau is 1 for Strong vs Full on reusability and 0.8 on verifiability.[^c10]
- Highest accuracy does not give highest reusability or verifiability: on GSM8K, DeepSeek and Gemma3 are most accurate yet Phi beats them by a large margin on both.[^c11]
- Kendall's tau between rankings from any two of accuracy, reusability and verifiability is low, so each captures a different aspect.[^c12]
- Specialized reasoning models were not consistently better than general-purpose Llama and Gemma.[^c13]

## Limits

- Executors are small (at most 3B) local models with default decoding; whether large Executors behave the same is untested here.
- Corrupted CoTs are generated by prompting; how corruption quality affects the score is not reported in what I read.
- Four Thinkers make rank correlations coarse (tau takes few values); the stability claim rests on that.
- Reusability conflates helpfulness with susceptibility: a gullible Executor scores the Thinker higher. This is my reading of the definition, not a tested point.
- Tasks are closed-answer reasoning benchmarks; nothing tests open-ended questions or whether the scores predict downstream multi-agent pipeline quality, which is only asserted.
- The measures test whether an answer changes with the trace, not whether the trace reflects the Thinker's internal computation.
