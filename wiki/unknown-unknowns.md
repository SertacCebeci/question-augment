# Unknown unknowns — the fourth knowledge state

> The one state of a question-asker's model that no question can be aimed at,
> because aiming requires the axis to exist first.

## The framing matrix

Donald Rumsfeld's DoD news briefing of 12 February 2002 (rumsfeld-2002) named[^s1][^s2][^s3][^s4][^s5]
three states — known knowns, known unknowns, unknown unknowns. The fourth cell,
the **unknown known**, is not his: Slavoj Žižek added it in 2004 (zizek-2004),
reading it as what we disavow knowing. The $2 \times 2$ shape itself is older —
Luft & Ingham's *Johari window* (luft-1955) crosses known/not-known to self with
known/not-known to others; the cells are not the same objects as Rumsfeld's, but
the grid form is theirs.

| | We know | We don't know |
|---|---|---|
| **We're aware** | known known | known unknown |
| **We're unaware** | unknown known | unknown unknown |

- **Known known** — in the model, single reading, confirmed
- **Known unknown** — a named empty slot. Can be asked directly
- **Unknown known** — read in this wiki as [[tacit-knowledge]]: known but unsaid
- **Unknown unknown** — this note's subject: cannot be asked for, only **hunted**

## Why an unknown unknown cannot be selected for

*This wiki's inference, not a published result.* Take the premise that a
question is chosen by a **criterion computed over a represented parameter space
$\theta$** — the [[expected-information-gain]] framing. EIG is defined only for
variables that appear in $\theta$. On an axis absent from $\theta$ the quantity
has no value at all, so **no criterion defined over $\theta$ can rank, and
therefore cannot produce, the question that would open that axis.** The
conclusion is about criteria of that form; it says nothing about generators that
do not score candidates against a model.

## Hunting techniques

| Technique | What it surfaces |
|---|---|
| [[grand-tour-question]] (Spradley 1979, spradley-1979) | the informant's structure, not the shape of your questions |
| Playback (retell in your own words) | the drift in your model |
| Edge/boundary walks | rules that definitions conceal |
| [[premortem]] (Klein 2007, klein-2007) | conditioning on failure |
| Negative space (compare to an analogue) | what every sibling has and this lacks |
| Perspective walk (change roles) | the region each role alone can see |
| "What should I have asked?" | the blind spot the other party can see |

What they share, on this note's reading: they change **the criterion for
generating questions**, not the criterion for selecting them.

## Transfer

| | |
|---|---|
| **Home field** | [[bayesian-experimental-design|Bayesian experimental design]]: EIG scores a candidate experiment by expected reduction in uncertainty over a **declared** parameter space $\theta$ with a prior on it ([[expected-information-gain]]). |
| **Here** | The "experiment" is a question and $\theta$ is the asker's model of $W$; the cell of interest is an axis that $\theta$ does not contain. |
| **What survives** | The definitional point: the score is a function of $\theta$, so it is undefined off $\theta$ — hence the impossibility above, for criteria of that form. |
| **What does not** | Any numeric claim. No prior, no likelihood and no measure over the unmodelled axis exists, so nothing here is computed; and the argument gives no procedure for finding the missing axis, only a reason why scoring will not. |

## Limits

- The impossibility argument holds **only** for selection criteria defined over
  a represented $\theta$. A generator that is not criterion-driven — a fixed
  protocol, an outside informant, an adversary — is outside the argument
  entirely, and the note does not show such a generator succeeds.
- The **generation / selection** split in the last line of *Hunting techniques*
  is an organising distinction of this note, not a measured one. No source cited
  here separates the two criteria experimentally.
- The $2 \times 2$ is a lens, not a measurement; no procedure classifies an item
  into a cell.
- Hunting techniques carry no completeness guarantee, by construction, and have
  not been compared against each other.
- "Unknown known" and "unknown unknown" are distinguishable only in retrospect.
- `[?]` luft-1955 — the 1955 *Proceedings of the Western Training Laboratory in
  Group Development* (UCLA Extension Office) is cited here from secondary
  literature; the primary text was not inspected.
