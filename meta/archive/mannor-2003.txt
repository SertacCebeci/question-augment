Computer Science ((LNAI,volume 2777)) 6229 Accesses 71 Citations Abstract We consider the
Multi-armed bandit problem under the PAC (“probably approximately correct”) model. It was shown by
Even-Dar et al. [5] that given n arms, it suffices to play the arms a total of
\(O\big(({n}/{\epsilon^2})\log ({1}/{\delta})\big)\) times to find an ε -optimal arm with
probability of at least 1- δ . Our contribution is a matching lower bound that holds for any
sampling policy. We also generalize the lower bound to a Bayesian setting, and to the case where the
statistics of the arms are known but the identities of the arms are not. This is a preview of
