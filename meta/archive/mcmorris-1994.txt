Analysis, and Knowledge Organization ((STUDIES CLASS)) 719 Accesses 17 Citations Summary The median
procedure for trees has been nicely characterized in a way that allows it to be efficiently
implemented. However, when the problem is restricted to binary trees, we will show that computing
the median binary tree is NP-haxd. This provides another reason to not always insist that a
“consensus tree” be fully resolved. This is a preview of subscription content, log in via an
