Explore all metrics Abstract We analyze the “query by committee” algorithm, a method for filtering
informative queries from a random stream of inputs. We show that if the two-member committee
algorithm achieves information gain with positive lower bound, then the prediction error decreases
exponentially with the number of queries. We show that, in particular, this exponential decrease
holds for query learning of perceptrons. Article PDF Download to read the full article text Similar
